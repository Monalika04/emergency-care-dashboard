# Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard

## Overview
Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze hospital emergency room operations and patient flow. This project provides actionable insights into patient admissions, wait times, department referrals, patient satisfaction, and overall hospital resource utilization.

The dashboard helps stakeholders monitor emergency room performance and make data-driven decisions to improve operational efficiency and patient care.

---

## Key Insights
- The emergency room handled 9,216 patients over 19 months
- Mondays recorded the highest patient volume
- Peak traffic hours occurred during 11 AM, 1 PM, 7 PM, and 11 PM
- Average patient wait time was 35.3 minutes
- General Practice received the highest referrals
- Adults aged 30–39 formed the largest patient group
- Admission and non-admission rates were nearly equal
